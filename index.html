Saurabh Kashid

I am an Autonomy Engineer focused on building robust, real world robotic systems. At Joulea in Atlanta, I design and deploy autonomy software for drone based building inspection including real time sensor fusion, LiDAR based obstacle detection, mission state machines, and path planning for safe, efficient coverage.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, lifting, and playing sports they keep me active, reset my brain, and give me fresh perspectives for tackling hard technical problems.

project page
